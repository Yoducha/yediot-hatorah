Yediot Hatorah Inc

Torah Learning Organization — Brooklyn, New York

Yediot Hatorah Inc is a nonprofit organization dedicated to Torah study. The organization supports a daily kollel and community learning programs, including group Talmud study for students learning multiple pages of Talmud each day.

Our mission is to strengthen daily Torah learning in the community and to support the scholars and students who devote themselves to it.
